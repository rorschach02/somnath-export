White fried onion

Light golden-white with a mild, slightly sweet flavour. Used in soups, gravies, instant noodles, bakery items and seasoning blends.

Red fried onion

Deep golden-brown and extra crispy with a strong onion flavour. The usual choice for biryani, pulao, curries and spice mixes.

Pink fried onion

Light golden with balanced pungency. Suits snacks, namkeen, frozen foods, catering and HoReCa.

Packing and storage

Packed in a food-grade poly liner inside multi-wall paper bags of 10, 20 or 25 kg. Custom bulk packing and private label are available.

Store sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Fried onion carries oil, so keep it away from heat and reseal opened bags to hold the crunch.

Questions about fried onion

What is the shelf life of fried onion?

9 to 12 months in a cool, dry place in the original sealed packing.

Which fried onion is best for biryani?

Red fried onion. It fries to a deep golden-brown and has the strongest flavour, so it holds up in biryani, pulao and curries.

Do you add colour or preservatives?

No. Our fried onion contains no artificial colour or preservatives.

What pack sizes are available?

10 kg, 20 kg and 25 kg multi-wall paper bags with a food-grade poly liner, or custom bulk packing under your label.
