Fried onion

Crispy, machine-sorted fried onion with no artificial colour or preservatives. Moisture max 3–5%, oil content 25–35%, shelf life 9–12 months.

White fried onion

Light golden-white with a mild, slightly sweet flavour. Used in soups, gravies, instant noodles and seasoning blends.

Red fried onion

Deep golden-brown and extra crispy with a strong onion flavour. The usual choice for biryani, pulao and curries.

Pink fried onion

Light golden with balanced pungency. Suits snacks, namkeen, frozen foods and catering.

From fresh bulb to sealed bag in ten steps

Every lot follows the same line in our Mahuva plant, so the cut and colour you approve on the sample is what arrives in the container.

  1. InspectFresh onion and garlic checked on arrival
  2. WashCleaned to remove soil and impurities
  3. PeelSkins and roots removed
  4. SliceCut to a uniform thickness
  5. DehydrateDried at low temperature in belt or tray dryers
  6. Cool and sortOff-colour pieces removed
  7. SizeMilled and sieved to the ordered cut
  8. Metal detectEvery lot screened
  9. SteriliseSteam sterilisation when the order requires it
  10. PackSealed in a controlled packing room

Questions buyers ask first

Do you send samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Tell us the product, cut and grade you need and we send a sample for your approval before you place a bulk order.

What is the shelf life of dehydrated onion and garlic?

12 to 24 months when stored in a cool, dry place in the original sealed packing. Fried onion keeps 9 to 12 months.

Which cuts and sizes do you supply?

Flakes 10–25 mm, chopped 3–6 mm and 6–8 mm, minced 1–3 mm and 2–4 mm, granules 0.2–0.5 mm and 0.5–1 mm, and powder at 80–100 mesh.

Do you offer private labelling and custom packing?

Yes. Standard packs are 14 kg and 20 kg bags with a food-grade poly liner. We also pack to your sizes and under your label.

Which countries do you export to?

We ship to buyers across Asia, Europe and the Middle East, and quote for other destinations on request.
