What importers in South Asia need to know

The main rules that affect a shipment of dehydrated vegetables or spices. Rules change, so confirm the details with your customs broker before you order.

Bangladesh

  • Many food imports need a certificate from the exporting country stating the food is fit for human consumption.
  • Production and expiry dates must be printed on each pack itself, not on a separate stuck-on label.
  • A certificate of origin can qualify the shipment for lower duty under the South Asian Free Trade Area (SAFTA).

Sri Lanka and Nepal

  • A certificate of origin under the India–Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement can reduce duty in Sri Lanka, depending on the product.
  • Shipments to Nepal move by road through the India–Nepal land border, usually with faster delivery than sea freight.
  • Check whether your product sits on the importing country's sensitive list, which excludes it from duty concessions.

India

  • We supply food manufacturers, seasoning companies, caterers and distributors across India under our FSSAI licence.
  • Domestic orders ship by road from Mahuva, in standard bulk bags or your own packing.

Last reviewed November 2026. This is general guidance, not legal advice.
