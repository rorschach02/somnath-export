Dehydrated pink onion manufacturer and exporter

Pink onion sits between white and red: more flavour than white, a little sweeter than red, and usually the most economical of the three for general food processing.

We make it in Mahuva, Gujarat, in five cuts from 25 mm flakes to 100 mesh powder, and ship to buyers across Asia, Europe and the Middle East.

Packing and storage

Flakes and chopped ship in 14 kg bags; minced, granules and powder in 20 kg bags, each with a food-grade poly liner. We also pack to your sizes and under your label.

Store sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and strong odours. Reseal opened bags, because dehydrated product absorbs moisture from the air.

About 8 to 10 kg of fresh onion goes into 1 kg of dehydrated onion.

Questions about dehydrated pink onion

Which cuts of dehydrated pink onion do you supply?

Flakes 10–25 mm, chopped 3–6 mm and 6–8 mm, minced 1–3 mm and 2–4 mm, granules 0.2–0.5 mm and 0.5–1 mm, and powder at 80–100 mesh. We can also cut to your size.

What is the shelf life of dehydrated pink onion?

12 to 24 months when stored in a cool, dry place away from sunlight, in the original sealed packing.

How do I use dehydrated pink onion in place of fresh?

About 8 to 10 kg of fresh onion goes into 1 kg of dehydrated onion. Soak pieces in warm water for 10 to 15 minutes to rehydrate, or add them straight to any recipe with enough liquid.
