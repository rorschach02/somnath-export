What importers in Africa need to know

The main rules that affect a shipment of dehydrated vegetables or spices. Rules change, so confirm the details with your customs broker before you order.

Nigeria

  • Processed food must be registered with NAFDAC before it can be imported or sold. The Nigerian importer or their agent holds the registration.
  • NAFDAC issues the registration number only after the product passes its quality tests, so allow time before the first shipment.

Kenya

  • Goods need a Certificate of Conformity under the Kenya Bureau of Standards PVoC programme, issued by an approved inspection company before the cargo leaves India.
  • Cargo that arrives without the certificate faces penalties and inspection at destination.

Other markets

  • Several other African countries, including Tanzania, Uganda and Egypt, run their own pre-shipment inspection or registration schemes. Your clearing agent will know the current one.
  • Halal certification is expected in North Africa and in Muslim-majority markets elsewhere on the continent.
  • South Africa needs labels that follow its food labelling regulations for retail packs.

Last reviewed November 2026. This is general guidance, not legal advice.
